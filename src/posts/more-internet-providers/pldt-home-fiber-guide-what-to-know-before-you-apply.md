Getting a wired internet connection at home is a major commitment. Before you send in an application for PLDT Home Fiber, you need to check a few practical details in your area. Taking the time to verify availability and requirements can save you from long waiting times and installation delays.

Here is a simple checklist to help you prepare before you apply.

## Check Area Availability First

Fiber internet relies on physical cables running directly to your home. Not every street, building, or subdivision has an available port in the nearest terminal box.

- Visit the official PLDT website or use their official app to check if your specific address is covered.
- Ask your neighbors who already use PLDT Home Fiber about their connection stability in your exact block.
- If you live in a condominium or a gated subdivision, check with your property management if PLDT lines are allowed and readily accessible.

## Review Current Plans and Prices

Internet providers regularly update their line-up of plans to match changing household needs. Because speeds and subscription costs change frequently, you should always look up current offers directly.

## Prepare Your Valid Identification

Like any formal utility application, setting up a broadband account requires identity verification. PLDT will ask for standard documents to process your subscription.

- Prepare at least one valid government-issued ID showing your current residential address.
- Have proof of billing ready, especially if your utility bills are under your name and match the installation address.
- If you are renting, check if your landlord requires specific paperwork for external installations that involve drilling or mounting equipment.

## Understand Installation and Setup

Once your application is approved, a team of technicians will visit your home to run the fiber cable and install the modem or router.

- Clear the area where you want the modem placed, ideally in a central spot in your home away from thick concrete walls.
- Ensure an adult is present at the installation address during the scheduled visit to inspect the setup and sign the turnover documents.
- Ask the technicians to test the connection speed on your devices before they leave so you can report any immediate issues.

## What to Check First Before You Decide

Before you submit your personal details, confirm that your budget can sustain the monthly subscription for the entire duration of the contract lock-in period. Check the official PLDT website or app to review their current plans, installation fees, and document requirements. Planning ahead ensures a smoother application process from submission to activation.
