A new DITO SIM has to be registered before you can use it for calls, texts and data. DITO says the whole process can be done on your phone in a few minutes. Here is what to prepare and how it works.

## Why you have to register

The SIM Registration Act (Republic Act No. 11934) requires every SIM to be tied to a verified identity. SIMs are sold deactivated and only switch on after a successful registration. This covers prepaid and postpaid users. According to DITO, a SIM that is not registered cannot be used for mobile services.

## What to prepare

- Your full name, date of birth, gender and address.
- A valid government-issued ID. DITO lists the accepted IDs on its SIM registration page.
- A phone camera for the identity check, which includes a selfie.

Some people have extra requirements, according to DITO:

- **Under 18.** The SIM is registered under a parent or legal guardian. You need the guardian's valid ID and their consent.
- **Businesses.** An SEC or DTI Certificate of Registration, plus a board resolution or special power of attorney that authorizes a representative.
- **Tourists.** Passport, proof of a local address and a return ticket showing the departure date. A tourist's registered SIM may be valid for a limited time only.
- **Foreigners on a non-tourist visa.** Passport, proof of address and documents such as an Alien Certificate of Registration or a school ID.

## How to register

1. **Insert the SIM** and check that the network name appears at the top of your screen. Connect your phone to WiFi so the registration page can open.
2. **Wait for DITO's text message** with the registration instructions, then tap the registration link inside it.
3. **Fill in the form.** Enter your personal details, upload your valid ID and take a selfie.
4. **Submit and save the confirmation.** You will get a confirmation message. Take a screenshot as proof that the SIM is registered.

> [!WARNING] Use official links only
> Register only through the link DITO sends to your SIM or through DITO's own SIM registration page. Never share your OTP, and do not upload your ID to links from strangers or social media.

## Common problems and fixes

- **OTP does not arrive.** Check your signal or internet connection, wait a few minutes before asking for a new code, and restart your phone if needed.
- **ID upload fails.** The cause is usually an unclear photo. Avoid glare and shadows, and make sure every line of text is readable.
- **The form is rejected.** Your entries must match your ID exactly. If the error continues, refresh the page and try again.
