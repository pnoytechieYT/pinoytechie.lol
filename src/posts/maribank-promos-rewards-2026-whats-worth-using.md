MariBank's app advertises several recurring perks alongside its savings rate. Some are genuinely useful for everyday users; others require enough conditions that the effective value is lower than the headline.

## The Referral Reward: Up to ₱588

This is the highest-value promo MariBank offers, but it requires action from both parties. The new user must enter a code, deposit **₱1,000 in one transaction**, and hold it for **three days** [citation:8]. The reward is paid the following week.

Worth using if you are opening an account anyway. Not worth opening an account solely for the ₱588 if you do not intend to use MariBank afterward—the holding requirement and the follow-up needed to confirm the payout add friction.

## Load and Data Discounts: Up to 5% Off

MariBank offers discounts on mobile load and data packages through the app, advertised at up to **5% off**. For a ₱100 load purchase, that is ₱5 saved.

Worth using if you already buy load regularly. The discount is small per transaction but recurring, and it requires no additional conditions beyond making the purchase through the MariBank app instead of another channel.

## Bill Payment Cashback: Up to ₱50 per Month

Paying bills through MariBank earns cashback up to **₱50 per month**. The exact percentage and eligible billers are shown in the app and can change.

Worth using if you have recurring bills that are eligible. ₱50 per month is ₱600 per year, which is not trivial for a feature that requires only changing where you click to pay. But check the eligible billers list—not every utility or credit card may qualify.

## ShopeePay Transfers: Free

Moving money from **ShopeePay to MariBank is free**. If you keep a balance in ShopeePay from Shopee purchases or refunds, this is a no-cost way to move it into an interest-earning account.

Worth using if you are a Shopee user. The value is in the free transfer and the interest on the moved balance, not a separate promo.

## What to Check Before Counting a Promo

**Expiry dates.** Promos have end dates, and MariBank can change terms at any time. A cashback offer that is active this month may not be next month.

**Eligibility conditions.** The referral reward's three-day holding requirement is the clearest example. Bill cashback may exclude certain billers. Load discounts may apply only to specific denominations.

**The in-app terms.** The bank sets the rules, and the app shows the current version. A blog post, including this one, is a snapshot. Before relying on a promo for a significant purchase, confirm the terms in the app at the moment you transact.

## The Honest Assessment

The referral reward is the only promo with a large one-time payout. The load and bill perks are small but real for users who already transact in those categories. None of them should be the reason to choose MariBank over another bank—the savings rate and free transfers are the core value. The promos are a bonus on top, not the foundation.
