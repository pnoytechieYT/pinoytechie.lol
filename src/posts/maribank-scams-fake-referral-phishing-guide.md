MariBank's safety page covers the basics—don't share your OTP, install only from official stores—but the specific scam patterns targeting digital bank users in the Philippines are more varied than that. Here are the ones that actually appear in the wild.

## Fake Referral Code Sellers

The scam: a page or individual offers a "premium" MariBank referral code for a fee, promising a higher reward or guaranteed payout.

The reality: **referral codes are free**. MariBank's official program pays up to ₱588 to both parties when the new user deposits ₱1,000 and holds it for three days [citation:8]. There is no premium tier, no paid code, and no legitimate reason to send money to anyone for a code. If a page asks for payment, it is not affiliated with MariBank.

## Phishing Pages That Copy the App

The scam: a link claims to be a MariBank login page or a "verification" portal and asks for your mobile number, password, or OTP.

The red flag: MariBank's app is the only place you should enter your credentials. The bank does not send links asking you to log in through a browser. If you receive an SMS or message with a link to "verify your account," do not click it. Open the app directly instead.

The official app is available only on **Google Play, the Apple App Store, and Huawei App Gallery** [citation:8]. Any APK file sent through a messaging app or downloaded from a third-party site is a risk.

## Impersonators on Social Media

The scam: an account posing as MariBank customer service responds to complaints or questions on Facebook or X, then asks for your account details to "help."

The red flag: legitimate bank support does not ask for your **password, PIN, or OTP** through social media. MariBank's official channels are listed in the app and on its website. If you need help, use the in-app support or the contact information published on maribank.ph.

## The "Account Will Be Suspended" Message

The scam: an SMS or email claims your MariBank account will be suspended unless you click a link and "re-verify" your identity.

The red flag: MariBank does not threaten suspension via unsolicited messages with links. Verification happens inside the app. If you are unsure, open the app and check your account status there. If there is a real issue, it will be visible when you log in.

## What Legitimate MariBank Communication Looks Like

MariBank sends OTPs for transactions you initiate. It does not send OTPs for "verification" you did not request. It credits interest and referral rewards directly to your account without asking you to claim them through a link.

The simplest rule: if a message asks you to do something urgently through a link, and you did not initiate it, it is not from MariBank. Open the app and check there.
