MariBank's headline rate looks simple, but the tiered structure and tax treatment matter if you are deciding where to park your cash.

## The Two Tiers

MariBank pays **3.25% per annum** on balances up to ₱1,000,000. For any amount above that threshold, the rate jumps to **3.75% per annum** [citation:1][citation:12]. The higher rate on excess balances is unusual—most banks apply a single rate or lower the rate for larger deposits—so it is worth understanding if you plan to keep a significant amount.

## Daily Crediting, Gross vs Net

Interest is credited to your account every midnight, based on your previous day's balance [citation:1]. What you see advertised is the **gross rate**. A **20% withholding tax** is deducted from the interest earned [citation:1][citation:12]. For example, if you maintain ₱50,000 for a full year at 3.25%, the gross interest is about ₱1,625, but the net amount after tax is lower.

## No Maintaining Balance

Unlike traditional banks, MariBank does not require a maintaining balance and has no lock-in period [citation:2][citation:12]. This makes it flexible for savings you might need to access, though the rate applies only as long as the balance sits in the account.

## Rate History

MariBank adjusted its rates before. The current 3.25% tier for balances up to ₱1M took effect on **January 15, 2026** [citation:12]. Rates are not guaranteed, so confirm the current figure in the app before moving money.
