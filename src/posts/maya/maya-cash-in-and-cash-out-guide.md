Adding and withdrawing money from your Maya account is simple once you know the available channels. Because fees and limits can change, always check the exact details directly inside the official app before making a transaction.

## Adding Money to Your Maya Wallet

You have several ways to fund your Maya wallet, ranging from digital transfers to physical partner stores. According to Maya, you can cash in through linked bank accounts, online banking portals, and partner touchpoints.

- **Linked bank accounts:** Free when you link your eligible bank account directly inside the app.
- **Over-the-counter partners:** Free up to a certain monthly threshold. According to Maya Bank, cash-in over the counter is free up to ₱8,000 a month, with extra charges applying to amounts above that limit.
- **Other e-wallets or banks:** You can transfer funds from other institutions via InstaPay or PESONet, though the sending bank may charge its own transfer fee.

> [!NOTE] Upgrade your account first
> Starter accounts have lower limits than upgraded ones. To enjoy higher caps and full access to Maya Savings, make sure you follow the steps in the [Maya account upgrade guide](/blog/how-to-open-and-upgrade-maya-account/).

## Moving Money to Maya Savings

Once funds are inside your Maya wallet, moving them into your interest-bearing deposit is straightforward. According to Maya Bank, wallet-to-savings transfers are instant and completely free.

| Action | What Maya Says | Fee / Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wallet to Savings | Instant | Free |
| Savings to Wallet | Instant | Free |
| Other Banks to Savings | Via InstaPay or PESONet | Sending bank sets the fee |

## Withdrawing and Sending Money Out

When you need to take money out of Maya, you can transfer it to other banks, send it to another user, or withdraw physical cash through supported channels. 

- **To other banks via InstaPay:** Transfers out of Maya Savings cost ₱15 per transaction.
- **To other banks via PESONet:** Transfers are free until further notice, though subject to clearing cutoffs.
- **Wallet cash out:** You can withdraw cash through partner agents, convenience stores, or supported ATMs using your physical card or app withdrawal features, noting any service fees charged by the partner.

## What to Check Before You Transact

Before you cash in or cash out, take a quick moment to verify current conditions:

1. **Check your limits:** View your remaining monthly cumulative limit in your profile to avoid failed transactions.
2. **Look at partner fees:** Physical partner stores and convenience outlets may impose service charges separate from Maya's own rules.
3. **Verify account status:** Ensure your account is fully verified so your transactions process smoothly without unexpected holds.
