## What counts as a first transaction

Maya's promo terms say the first transaction needs a minimum spend of **₱100**, and any one of these counts:

- Buying select load or data products in **Shop** (for yourself only, not as a gift)
- Paying a bill through **Bills Pay**
- Depositing to **Maya Savings** from your wallet
- Paying with **Maya QR** (except Negosyo QR merchants)
- Paying to a registered Maya number
- Paying with your Maya virtual or physical card, online or in a store

The upgrade and the first transaction can happen in either order.
