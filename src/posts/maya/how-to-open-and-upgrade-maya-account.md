Opening a Maya account takes a few minutes. The step that matters most is the **upgrade**, because many features, including Maya Savings, are only available to upgraded accounts.

## What you need

- A smartphone and a **Philippine mobile number** that can receive an OTP
- The Maya app from the **official Google Play Store, Apple App Store or Huawei AppGallery**
- **One valid ID** for the upgrade (Maya lists the accepted IDs at maya.ph/upgrade)

## Step 1: Create the account

1. Install Maya and tap sign up.
2. Enter your mobile number and the OTP Maya sends you.
3. Fill in your details and set your PIN.
4. If you have a referral code, enter it when the app asks. See the [Maya referral code guide](/blog/maya-referral-code-guide-50-reward/) for the current promo.

You now have a **starter** account. It works for basic payments, but with lower limits and fewer features.

## Step 2: Upgrade your account

Upgrading is free. Maya says it unlocks the rest of its features and that you only need one valid ID.

Have these details ready, because Maya Bank asks for them when you open Maya Savings:

1. Full name, including your middle name, and/or your PhilSys number if you have one
2. Date and place of birth
3. Address
4. Contact number and email address
5. Citizenship or nationality
6. Source of funds and nature of work
7. Signature or biometric data
8. One primary ID, or two secondary IDs

Scan your ID inside the app and follow the prompts. Bad lighting and a blurry photo are the usual reasons an upgrade is rejected, so use a flat surface and good light.

> [!TIP] Make the name match
> Enter your name exactly as it appears on the ID. A mismatch is a common cause of failed verification.

## What the upgrade unlocks

- **Maya Savings** and its Personal Goals and Time Deposit Plus accounts
- A higher account limit. Maya's promo terms mention aggregate limits of ₱50,000 for starter accounts and ₱100,000 for upgraded accounts, so check your own limits in the app because they can change
- Your own **@username** and the Refer and Earn feature
- Access to Maya's other services such as loans and cards, subject to approval

## Putting money in

According to Maya Bank, cash-in to your Maya Wallet is:

- **Free** from your linked bank accounts
- **Free up to ₱8,000 a month** at over-the-counter channels

Moving money from your wallet into Maya Savings is instant and free. Deposits from other banks and e-wallets arrive through InstaPay or PESONet, and the fee depends on the sending bank.

## After you are set up

- Turn on app security and biometrics.
- Never share your OTP, PIN or password. Maya does not ask for them.
- Keep your phone number and email up to date. Maya requires you to report changes in your information.
