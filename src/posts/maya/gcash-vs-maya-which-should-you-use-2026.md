GCash and Maya are both e-wallets, and both let you save through a partner or sister bank. They are close enough that the real question is not which one is better, but which one fits the job.

## The short answer

- **Pick Maya for savings** if you will upgrade and use it for regular payments, because the interest ceiling is much higher.
- **Pick GCash for its wallet** if you already pay, send and receive money there, and keep your savings wherever the rate is best.
- **Using both is fine.** Moving money between them is possible, and Maya Bank accepts deposits from GCash.

> [!NOTE] Wallet balances are not insured
> Money sitting in the GCash or Maya wallet is e-money. PDIC insurance, up to ₱1,000,000 per depositor, covers the **bank deposit** once money is moved into GSave or Maya Savings.

## Savings: where the gap is

On the base rate alone, Maya's 3% beats CIMB GSave's 2.30%. On ₱10,000 for a year that is about ₱240 against ₱184 after the 20% tax, a difference of about ₱56.

The bigger difference is the ceiling. Maya advertises up to 15% on balances up to ₱100,000, but that depends on boosts and activity. GSave also offers other banks. UNO pays a higher rate than CIMB, so the partner you pick matters more than the app. Read the [GSave guide](/blog/gcash-gsave-guide-rates-and-partner-banks/) before you choose.

## Everyday use

Both apps cover bills, load, QR payments, card payments and bank transfers. The fee that adds up is the bank transfer: ₱10 on GCash and ₱15 on Maya Savings per InstaPay transfer. If you send money to banks often, plan fewer, larger transfers.

## Who should use what

- **You want the simplest setup:** one wallet, with savings parked at one partner. Either app works.
- **You chase interest:** upgrade Maya, read the boost conditions and treat 3% as the number you can count on.
- **You get paid or receive money often:** keep your account fully verified so limits do not get in the way. See [how to verify GCash](/blog/how-to-open-and-fully-verify-gcash-account/).
- **You also use MariBank:** the [MariBank vs GCash comparison](/blog/maribank-vs-gcash/) covers how a pure digital bank fits next to these wallets.
