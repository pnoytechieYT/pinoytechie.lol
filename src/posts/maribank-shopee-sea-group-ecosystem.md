MariBank is not a standalone startup bank. It is part of **Sea Group**, the company that also owns Shopee and Garena [citation:2][citation:5]. That ownership shapes the product in ways that matter for everyday users.

## Free Transfers from ShopeePay

You can move money from **ShopeePay to MariBank for free** [citation:12]. If you already use Shopee for shopping and keep a balance in ShopeePay, this is a low-friction way to consolidate funds into an interest-earning account.

## In-App Perks

MariBank's app offers discounts on load and data packages (up to 5% off) and cashback on bill payments (up to ₱50 per month) [citation:2]. These are small but recurring benefits that reduce the friction of using the app for everyday transactions.

## Why Forbes Ranked It #1

In the **Forbes World's Best Banks 2026** list, MariBank took the top spot in the Philippines [citation:5][citation:10]. The ranking was based on customer satisfaction across trustworthiness, terms and conditions, customer service, digital services, and quality of financial advice. MariBank's integration with Shopee allowed it to acquire customers at lower cost than traditional banks, which it then passed on through higher rates and free services [citation:5].

## The Catch

Ecosystem perks are real, but they are not a reason to keep more than ₱1,000,000 in the bank if you are concerned about insurance limits. And rates can change—MariBank has adjusted them before [citation:12]. Treat the ecosystem as a convenience bonus, not a substitute for the fundamentals.
