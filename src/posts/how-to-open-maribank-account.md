MariBank advertises a 5-minute account opening process [citation:2]. In practice, preparation determines whether it actually takes 5 minutes or 20.

## What You Need

- A smartphone with the MariBank app installed from the **official Google Play Store or Apple App Store**. Do not install APKs from links.
- A valid **government-issued ID** for verification.
- Your **personal details**: full name, birthdate, address, and contact information.
- A **selfie** for liveness verification.

## The Steps

1. Open the app and select **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your mobile number and verify with the OTP sent to you.
3. Scan your ID and take a selfie when prompted.
4. Fill in your personal information.
5. If you have a referral code, enter it during sign-up. A referral code is free—nobody should charge you for one.
6. Set your password and PIN. Do not share these with anyone.

## Common Snags

- **ID quality**: Glare, blur, or expired IDs cause verification failures. Use good lighting and a flat surface.
- **Name mismatch**: Your name in the app must match your ID exactly.
- **Network issues**: The liveness check requires a stable connection. Switch to Wi-Fi if mobile data fails.

## After Opening

Your account number is generated immediately. You can start transferring money in and earning interest the same day. If you want the physical MariCard, order it in the app (₱49 fee) [citation:12].
