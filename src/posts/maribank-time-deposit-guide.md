MariBank's savings account is the headline product, but it also offers a time deposit for users who want to lock in a rate for a short period.

## The Basics

You can open a time deposit with as little as **₱100**. The term options are **1 month or 3 months**, and the annual rate is **3.75%** [citation:2]. Interest is credited **immediately upon successful placement** of the deposit, not at maturity [citation:2]. This is different from many traditional time deposits that pay at the end of the term.

## Auto-Renewal

You can opt for automatic renewal, so when the term ends, the principal (and potentially the interest, depending on your instructions) rolls into a new term of the same length [citation:2]. This is convenient if you want to keep the money parked without manually reopening each cycle.

## Time Deposit vs Savings

The savings account pays 3.25% on balances up to ₱1M and is fully liquid [citation:1]. The time deposit pays 3.75% but locks your money for the term. The trade-off is liquidity for a modest rate premium. For money you will not touch for a month or three, the time deposit is straightforward.
