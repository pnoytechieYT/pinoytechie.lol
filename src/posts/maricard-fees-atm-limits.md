MariBank's debit card, MariCard, is free to get (the physical card costs ₱49), but it comes with specific fee structures that are easy to miss until you are at an ATM or paying in a foreign currency.

## Local ATM Withdrawals

Withdrawing from a local ATM costs **₱15** per transaction, though if the ATM itself charges a fee, only the ATM's fee applies. Balance inquiries at local ATMs cost **₱5**. Daily withdrawal limits vary by account standing, so check the Card Settings section in the app rather than assuming a fixed amount.

## International ATM Withdrawals and FX

International ATM cash withdrawals are **free** on MariBank's side, but some overseas ATMs may impose their own transaction fee. International balance inquiries cost **₱50**.

For card purchases, the FX treatment depends on where you tap or click:
- **Overseas in-store purchases**: **0% foreign transaction fee**.
- **Overseas online transactions**: **2% foreign transaction fee** (since March 4, 2025).

This distinction matters if you subscribe to services billed in USD or shop from international sellers online.

## Transfers

MariBank offers **unlimited free transfers** via InstaPay and PESONet. This is a meaningful perk if you move money between banks frequently.
