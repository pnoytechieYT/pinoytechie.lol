MariBank and GCash get compared a lot, but they are not the same kind of app. GCash is an e-wallet built for paying and sending money. MariBank is a digital bank built for keeping money. Most people end up using both.

## The short answer

- Use **GCash** for bills, QR payments, and daily spending.
- Use **MariBank** for savings you will not touch soon, since it pays a higher interest rate and its deposits are PDIC-insured.

## Side by side

| | MariBank | GCash |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Digital bank (formerly SeaBank PH) | E-wallet; GSave savings sit with a partner bank |
| PDIC coverage | Insured up to ₱1,000,000 per depositor | Wallet balance is e-money, not a bank deposit; GSave follows its partner bank |
| Savings rate | 3.25% a year up to ₱1,000,000 (since January 15, 2026) | Lower than MariBank in 2026; check GSave in the app |
| Best for | Parking savings | Paying bills, QR, daily spending |

Rates change often. MariBank itself cut its rate in January 2026, so confirm the current number in the app before you move money. Published rates are gross, and 20% withholding tax comes off the interest.

## Savings: where MariBank is ahead

MariBank's savings account pays 3.25% a year on balances up to ₱1,000,000 and 3.75% on the portion above that. There is no maintaining balance, and interest is credited daily. GCash's savings feature, GSave, is a partner-bank deposit, and the rates published for it in 2026 were lower.

## Safety: what PDIC covers

MariBank Philippines is a licensed bank, so your deposit is insured by PDIC up to ₱1,000,000 per depositor. A GCash wallet balance is e-money rather than a bank deposit, so it is not covered the same way. For GSave, coverage depends on the partner bank. Read [is MariBank legit](/blog/is-maribank-legit/) for the full safety checklist.

## Everyday spending: where GCash is ahead

GCash is accepted in far more places: QR payments in stores, bill payments, load, and more. MariBank is built around saving and transfers, and it advertises free transfers. You can also move money from ShopeePay to MariBank for free, which is handy if you shop on Shopee.

## How to use both

Keep a small spending balance in GCash. Park the rest in MariBank, and move money across when you need it. This is a general comparison, not financial advice, so check your own fees and limits in each app.
