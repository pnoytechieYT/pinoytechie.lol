Every MariBank guide correctly states that deposits are insured by the PDIC up to **₱1,000,000 per depositor** [citation:3]. What most guides do not explain is what to do when your savings exceed that amount.

## The Rule

PDIC insurance applies **per depositor, per bank**. If you have ₱1,500,000 in a single MariBank savings account, ₱1,000,000 is insured and ₱500,000 is not. If you spread that ₱1,500,000 across two banks—₱750,000 in MariBank and ₱750,000 in another PDIC-insured bank—the full amount is covered.

The coverage is not reduced by having accounts at multiple banks. Each bank's coverage is separate.

## The Practical Split

For a saver with **₱2,000,000** in cash savings, a simple structure looks like this:

- **MariBank**: ₱1,000,000 at 3.25% (fully insured)
- **Second bank**: ₱1,000,000 at whatever rate that bank offers (fully insured)

Both accounts are covered up to the limit, and you capture MariBank's rate on the first million while diversifying the second million to another institution's rate.

If you have **₱3,000,000**, you need three banks, or two banks with one holding ₱2,000,000 (of which only ₱1,000,000 is insured). The uninsured portion is not "lost"—it is a claim on the bank—but in a failure scenario, recovery depends on asset liquidation and is not guaranteed at face value.

## The Rate Trade-Off

Splitting across banks means you cannot put everything in the highest-rate account. If Tonik pays 4.00% and MariBank pays 3.25%, keeping ₱1,000,000 in each instead of ₱2,000,000 in Tonik costs you the rate difference on the second million.

That cost is the price of insurance. Whether it is worth paying depends on your risk tolerance. For most retail savers with balances near the ₱1M threshold, the insurance is worth more than the extra 0.75% on the excess.

## What Counts as a Separate Bank

Different brands under the same banking license do not count as separate banks for PDIC purposes. CIMB's UpSave and GSave products are both under CIMB Bank's license, so ₱600,000 in UpSave and ₱600,000 in GSave at the same time would be ₱1,200,000 at one bank, with only ₱1,000,000 insured [citation:3].

MariBank Philippines and MariBank Singapore are separate legal entities in separate jurisdictions with separate insurance schemes. A deposit in MariBank Philippines is covered by PDIC; a deposit in MariBank Singapore is covered by Singapore's deposit insurance scheme. They do not combine.

## The Simplest Rule

For any balance above ₱1,000,000, count how much you have at each bank. If any single bank holds more than ₱1M of your money, move the excess to another PDIC-insured institution. The 20% withholding tax on interest applies regardless of how you split, so the tax treatment does not change.
