A new Globe or TM SIM will not work for calls, texts or data until you register it. Registration is free, takes a few minutes, and Globe uses the same system for both brands. Here is what you need and how it works.

## Why you have to register

The SIM Registration Act (Republic Act No. 11934), signed in October 2022, requires every SIM, including eSIMs, to be registered before it can be used. According to Globe, SIMs are sold in a deactivated state and only switch on once the owner registers. The law's aim is to cut down on scam and spam texts.

## What you need before you start

- Your Globe or TM number, on a phone that can receive texts, because Globe sends a one-time PIN (OTP) there.
- Your full name, birthday, gender, address and nationality.
- A valid, unexpired government ID with your photo.
- A selfie, which you take during registration.

Globe's current list of accepted IDs includes the passport, driver's license, National ID (PhilID or ePhilID), BIR or TIN ID, UMID, SSS or MySSS ID, PhilHealth ID, PRC ID, postal ID and voter's ID. The list can change, so check Globe's registration page before you start.

## How to register

**Option 1: the GlobeOne app.** Open the app and tap **Register Now**, choose the Globe account or enter your mobile number, then wait for the confirmation.

**Option 2: Globe's SIM registration website.**

1. Open Globe's official SIM registration page.
2. Enter your 10-digit mobile number (for example 9171234567) and tap **Register**.
3. Wait for the OTP text message.
4. Enter the 6-digit OTP. It expires after 5 minutes.
5. Fill in the form: name, birthday, gender, address and nationality.
6. Take a selfie, choose your ID type and upload a copy of the ID.
7. Tick the box to accept the privacy notice and confirm that your details are complete and accurate.
8. Tap **Submit**.
9. Keep the reference number shown on screen as proof of registration.

> [!WARNING] Use official channels only
> Register only on Globe's own website or in the GlobeOne app. Never share your OTP, and do not upload your ID through links sent by strangers or found on social media.

## Good to know

- **When to register.** Right away if you want to activate and use the SIM. If you will not use it yet, Globe says you have until the shelf-life expiry date printed on the SIM packaging.
- **Several SIMs.** Register each one under your own name.
- **Minors.** A SIM for a minor is registered under the parent or legal guardian.
- **Registration does not keep a SIM alive.** A registered SIM can still expire if you do not reload it regularly.
- **Postpaid.** Globe says the details from your application are already in its system, but it may contact you to confirm them.
- **GCash is separate.** Being [fully verified on GCash](/blog/how-to-open-and-fully-verify-gcash-account/) does not register your SIM. Globe's own FAQ says SIM registration must be done separately.

## What happens if you do not register

A new SIM stays deactivated. For a SIM that misses its deadline, Globe says it is deactivated permanently, and you can no longer use calls, texts or data on it.
