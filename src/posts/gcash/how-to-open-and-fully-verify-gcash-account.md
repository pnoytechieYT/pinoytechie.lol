A new GCash account starts as a **Basic** profile with small limits. Getting **Fully Verified** removes most of those limits and unlocks savings, credit and investment features. It takes a government ID and a selfie.

## Basic vs Fully Verified

GCash's Help Center says you check your own limits in the app under **Profile, then Profile Limits**, because limits can change. As of the October 1, 2026 update, a Basic profile looks like this:

| | Basic | Fully Verified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wallet limit | ₱10,000 | Much higher. See the [fees and limits guide](/blog/gcash-fees-and-wallet-limits-2026/) |
| Money in per month | ₱5,000 | Much higher |
| Money out per month | ₱5,000 | Much higher |
| GSave, GInvest, GInsure, loans | Not available | Available, subject to each product's rules |

If you receive your salary, a remittance or payments from customers in GCash, verify first. A Basic account stops receiving once ₱5,000 has come in during the month.

## What you need

- You are a Filipino citizen aged 18 or above (GCash Jr. exists for ages 7 to 17)
- A Philippine SIM registered to you
- A valid government-issued ID. The National ID works
- A phone camera for a selfie

## Step 1: Create the account

1. Download **GCash** from the official Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
2. Register with your mobile number and enter the OTP.
3. Set your MPIN. This is your security code, so never share it.

## Step 2: Get Fully Verified

1. Open GCash and log in.
2. Tap **Profile** and select **Verify Now**.
3. Tap **Get Started** and read the requirements.
4. Choose your ID type and scan the ID. Use good light and keep it flat.
5. Take your selfie when prompted and confirm your personal details.
6. Submit, then wait for GCash to confirm.

## Why verification fails

- **Blurry, glared or cropped ID.** Retake it in daylight.
- **Name or birthday that does not match the ID.** Enter it exactly as printed.
- **Expired ID or an ID type that is not accepted.** Try another valid ID.
- **You already have the maximum number of accounts.** An adult can have up to five GCash accounts, which are linked to one profile.

If GCash asks for more documents, you can resubmit within **10 days**. After you do, GCash advises waiting about four days. If the window passes, you may have to start the process again. For anything stuck, use **Help, then Get Help** in the app.

## After you are verified

- Open the savings you want in [GSave](/blog/gcash-gsave-guide-rates-and-partner-banks/).
- Check **Profile Limits** to see your real wallet and monthly limits.
- Turn on biometrics and in-app OTP notifications.

> [!WARNING] GCash never asks for your MPIN or OTP
> Scammers pose as GCash support, "ID verification services" and fixers on social media. GCash, banks and government agencies do not ask for your MPIN or OTP by call, text or chat. If money is taken, report it through the GCash Help Center right away.
