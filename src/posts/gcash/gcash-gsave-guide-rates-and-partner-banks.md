Your GCash wallet pays no interest. **GSave** is the part of the app that lets you open a real savings account with a partner bank, without leaving GCash.

## How GSave works

You open a savings account with a partner bank through GCash, then move money from your wallet into it. The money in GSave is a **deposit at that partner bank**, so it earns interest and is covered by PDIC insurance up to **₱1,000,000 per depositor per bank**.

You need a **Fully Verified** GCash account first. See [how to get verified](/blog/how-to-open-and-fully-verify-gcash-account/).

## CIMB GSave: the details

- **Rate:** 2.30% a year, credited monthly, less 20% tax.
- **Starts earning:** from the first centavo.
- **Deposit limit:** ₱100,000 a year until you upgrade the account.
- **Withdrawal limit:** ₱100,000 a day.
- **Eligibility:** 18 or older, a Filipino citizen, not a US person, with a valid government ID and a Fully Verified GCash account.

CIMB also runs an **Earn More** promotion until December 31, 2026 that can add interest for customers whose average daily balance grows month on month with money from outside CIMB. It is conditional, so read the terms before you count on it.

### A worked example

₱10,000 kept for a year at 2.30%:

- Gross interest: ₱230
- Less 20% tax: ₱46
- You keep about **₱184**

## How to open GSave

1. Make sure your GCash is **Fully Verified**.
2. Open GCash and tap **GSave**.
3. Compare the partner banks and tap the one you want.
4. Follow the registration steps and confirm your details.
5. Move money from your wallet into the new account.

Deposits and withdrawals through GSave are free.

## Things to watch

- **Dormancy:** partner banks charge a ₱30 dormancy fee after a period of inactivity, which is usually up to 24 months. Keep an eye on accounts you stop using.
- **Separate limits:** GSave has its own limits on top of your wallet limits.
- **Interest is not guaranteed.** Banks change rates and give notice, so check the rate before every large deposit.
- **Insurance is per bank.** If you split savings across several banks, each is covered separately up to ₱1,000,000.
