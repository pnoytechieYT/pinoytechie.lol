Two things changed for GCash users in 2026: the **InstaPay fee** went down, and the **wallet and transaction limits** were raised on October 1. Here is what each means for you.

## GCash fees

The figures below come from GCash's fee schedule as summarised by PHBanks in November 2026. The InstaPay change is confirmed by an advisory in the GCash Help Center.

| Transaction | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Bank transfer via InstaPay | ₱10 per transfer, since July 4, 2026 (previously ₱15) |
| Over-the-counter cash-in at partner stores | Free up to ₱8,000 a month, then 2% of the excess |
| Cash-in from a linked bank | ₱5 from UnionBank, ₱15 from BPI, ₱5 to ₱25 from other banks |
| Cash-out at partner outlets | 2% of the amount |
| GCash Card | ₱250 for the card, plus the ATM's own fee when you withdraw |
| GSave deposits and withdrawals | Free |
| QR payments | Free |

### A quick example

Depositing ₱12,000 in cash at a partner store in a month costs 2% of the part above ₱8,000. That is 2% of ₱4,000, or **₱80**. Splitting the deposit across two months would avoid it.

### How to keep fees low

- Keep money in GCash for spending, and pay by QR.
- Send fewer, larger bank transfers rather than many small ones. Five transfers a month at ₱10 is ₱600 a year.
- Cash in from a linked bank instead of over the counter when the bank's own fee is lower.

## Wallet and transaction limits

GCash says its wallet limits were updated effective **October 1, 2026**. Update the app to see the new limits.

| Profile | Wallet limit | Money in or out per month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic (not verified) | ₱10,000 | ₱5,000 |
| GCash Jr. (verified, ages 7 to 17) | ₱100,000 | ₱100,000 |
| Fully Verified adult | Raised substantially from the old ₱100,000 | Raised substantially |

> [!NOTE] Check your own limit
> Reports on the exact Fully Verified figure differ. One news report of the update lists ₱500,000, while other summaries list ₱1,000,000. GCash's Help Center says limits depend on your profile, so open **Profile, then Profile Limits** to see the number that applies to you.

### How the limits behave

- **Limits reset on the first day of the following month.**
- **Incoming and outgoing are counted separately.**
- An adult can have up to **five GCash accounts**. They are linked to one profile, and the **highest** limit applies to all of them. Limits are **shared, not added**, so a second number does not give you a second allowance.
- Some services, including the **GCash Card, GSave and GCrypto**, have their own limits on top of your wallet limits.
- GCash can change or pause limits to follow BSP rules, prevent fraud and protect accounts.

### If you hit a limit

You cannot use certain services until the limit resets or you upgrade your profile. If your verification is already complete and your limit looks wrong, use **Help, then Get Help** in the app.

## Is money in the wallet insured

No. The GCash wallet holds e-money, which is not a bank deposit and is not PDIC-insured. Savings you move into [GSave](/blog/gcash-gsave-guide-rates-and-partner-banks/) are deposits at the partner bank, which are insured up to ₱1,000,000 per depositor per bank.
