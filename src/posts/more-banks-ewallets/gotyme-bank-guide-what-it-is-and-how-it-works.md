## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many beginners make small errors that delay their account verification or expose them to security risks. Watch out for these common pitfalls:

- **Using blurry photos.** If your ID photo is dark or blurry, the automated system will reject it, forcing you to retake the pictures.
- **Typing mismatched details.** Ensure that the name and birthday you type into the app match your government ID exactly.
- **Sharing your OTP.** Never give your One-Time Password or PIN to anyone who calls or messages you claiming to be a bank representative.
